- **platform-stack:** `argo-events-sa`, the ServiceAccount of the `redteam-run-sensor` Sensor, no longer has the cluster-wide `argo-events-role` (Secrets, `pods/exec`, pods, ConfigMaps, Services, PVCs, Deployments, StatefulSets, Leases and the Argo Events resources in every namespace). It keeps `argo-events-role-workflow` through a RoleBinding in `redteam-jobs` only, which is what the Sensor needs to submit its Workflows. On upgrade, Helm deletes the ClusterRole `argo-events-role` and the ClusterRoleBindings `argo-events-role` and `argo-events-sa-argo-event`, and replaces the ClusterRoleBinding `argo-events-binding-workflow` with a RoleBinding of the same name in `redteam-jobs`. No values change.
